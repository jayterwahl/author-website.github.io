Many parts were embarrassing or cringe. Some parts were too woke, as y'all were moved by tides of moral fashion. Many parts were over the top, and sometimes bad.

Also there was a thread of coming to accept and forgive yourself. It was raw in its commitment to telling the whole, abjectly humiliating truth. And loving yourself anyway.

Thank you. It healed me a little. I would be proud to have made it.
